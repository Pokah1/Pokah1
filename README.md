### 🛠️ Featured Projects

**[Assessly](https://assessly-cbt.vercel.app/)** — Full-stack CBT (Computer-Based Test) recruitment portal built for Nigerian banks and organisations as an affordable, secure alternative to expensive third-party platforms or manual paper testing. Candidates register, pick a qualification category, and sit a timed browser-based test with server-side timing, per-candidate question/option shuffling, and tab-switch detection. Results are auto-scored and emailed instantly; admins get a full dashboard to review submissions.
- **Stack:** Next.js 15 (App Router, RSC) · Supabase (PostgreSQL, RLS) · Resend
- **Notable engineering:** server-side timer (browser never owns the clock), Fisher-Yates shuffling per candidate, 3-strike tab-switch auto-submission
- ⚠️ *Live demo runs on Supabase's free tier, which pauses after 7 days of inactivity — first load may take ~30s to spin back up.*

**Smart Tab Manager** — Chrome/Firefox extension (Manifest V3, React 18, Vite 5) for tab grouping by domain, duplicate detection, a site blocker, and a built-in Pomodoro timer. *(Not yet published to a store — repo available on request.)*
